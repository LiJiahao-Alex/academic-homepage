Jiahao Li

Assistant Professor · Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences

I study how machine learning systems can detect unfamiliar patterns and how data owners can protect their data from unauthorized training. My current work focuses on memory mechanisms for anomaly detection and on unlearnable data.

lijiahao@ict.ac.cn Google Scholar

Research

  • Memory and anomaly detection. Learning representations of normal behavior to detect anomalies in images and multivariate time series.
  • Data protection for machine learning. Designing unlearnable data that resists unauthorized training, including when images are transformed.
  • Open-world and robust perception. Building models that can handle unfamiliar inputs in real-world settings.

I welcome research collaborations and inquiries from students interested in machine learning, computer vision, or representation learning. Please email me with a brief introduction and the research questions or topics you would like to discuss.
